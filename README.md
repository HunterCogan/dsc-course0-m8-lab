# Aviation Accident Analysis

## Project Overview
Analysis of 17,225 aviation accidents (1983-2023) to identify the safest aircraft makes/models and key safety factors.

## Key Findings

### Safest Aircraft
**Small Aircraft:** Cessna, Beechcraft, Piper
**Large Aircraft:** Boeing, Airbus, McDonnell Douglas

### Recommended Models
**Small:** Cessna 172, Beechcraft Baron, Piper Cherokee
**Large:** Boeing 737, Airbus A320, Boeing 777

## Critical Safety Factors

### Factor 1: Weather Conditions
- **VMC (clear weather):** ~3% injury rate
- **IMC (poor weather):** ~9% injury rate
- Clear weather significantly reduces destruction rates

### Factor 2: Number of Engines
- **Single-engine:** 6% injury rate, 45% destruction
- **Twin-engine:** 4% injury rate, 25% destruction
- **Four-engine:** 2% injury rate, 15% destruction
- Multi-engine redundancy saves lives

## Recommendations
1. Prioritize multi-engine aircraft
2. Avoid flying in poor weather conditions
3. Focus on modern Boeing and Airbus models
